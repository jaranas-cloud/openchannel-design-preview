// WHAT IT DOES

One portal for the numbers, codes and proxies behind your accounts.

Five parts, one login. Your account manager switches each part on for your team.

01 · NUMBERS & CODESLIVE

A number for every account. Codes land in your portal.

Each account gets its own number. Verification codes from Facebook, Google and others arrive in your portal, hidden until you press Show.

02 · PROXY CHANGELIVE

Swap the proxy behind a profile.

Pick a profile and press Change proxy. We put in a spare from the same state, from whichever provider has one, and your table shows the new line.

03 · GET A NUMBERON REQUEST

Pull a new number yourself.

Search and buy inside the limits your account manager sets: a total and a daily cap. Every pull is logged.

04 · YOUR ACCOUNTON REQUEST

Ad spend, billing and requests in one place.

Dashboard, Meta ad spend, subscriptions, recurring billing, ad accounts, whitelisted domains and support tickets sit in the same portal.

05 · TEAM ACCESSLIVE

Invite your people.

Account managers send invite links. Each person signs in with Google, and the team decides which pages your company can see.

// QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

Who is OpenChannel for?
Agencies and teams that run many social media and ad accounts for clients, and need a phone number and a clean IP behind each one.
How do I get access?
OpenChannel is invite-only. Your account manager sends you a link. No account manager yet? Send a request above and the team will get back to you.
What does it cost?
Pricing depends on how many numbers and proxies you run. [PRICING COPY: Ryan and Dan to confirm before launch.]
Who can see my codes?
Only people on your team. Codes are hidden until someone presses Show, and each reveal is logged.
Can I get numbers and proxies myself?
Yes. Pulling numbers is switched on per company, with a total and a daily limit. Changing a proxy is a button in your profile table.
Which providers are behind it?
Numbers and proxies come from several providers. You never have to choose between them: a swap stays in the same state, whoever has a spare.
