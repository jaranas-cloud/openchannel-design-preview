// WHAT IS SYFTCOM

What is SyftCom?

SyftCom is an agency that provides Facebook ad accounts at scale. We set up the accounts, assign you an account manager, and give you a client portal where ad spend, billing and support requests live together. It is built for teams that need many accounts, not one.

What we provide
Facebook ad accounts, set up and handed over to your team.
Who it is for
Agencies, media buyers and growth teams that run ads across many accounts.
What you get
An account manager and a client portal for ad spend, billing and requests.
How to start
