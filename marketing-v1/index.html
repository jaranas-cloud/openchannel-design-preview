What is OpenChannel?

OpenChannel is a client workspace for agencies and teams managing multiple accounts. It brings assigned phone numbers and verification codes together, with proxies, services, billing, and other tools available when enabled for your account.

How do I get access?

Use the invitation link from your account manager. You can sign in with Google or use the email option provided in your invitation. A new Google sign-in without an invitation sends a request for the team to review.

Will I see every tool when I sign in?

Your workspace is configured for your account. Your account manager enables the tools and services you need, so available pages and actions can differ between clients.

Can I request more numbers or change a proxy?

These actions depend on the features enabled for your account and the limits set by the team. If the option is not available in your workspace, contact your account manager.

Where do I find pricing?

Your account manager can confirm pricing for your services and setup. Contact them to discuss what you need and which tools should be included in your workspace.
